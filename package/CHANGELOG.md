## v1.1.0

- **Preorder Catalog:** press **P** in the shop to browse items by category, with prices. Click and confirm to preorder.
- **PREORDERED - PAID** tag on the delivered item, instead of a $0 price.
- **Hover hints** on shop items: "Not what you wanted? Press [P] to preorder something else."
- Other players with the mod see the hints and tags too.
- New setting: **Controls → CatalogKey** (default P).
- Needs **MenuLib** for the catalog. Chat commands still work without it.

## v1.0.2

- Unfinished (work-in-progress) items can no longer be preordered.

## v1.0.1

- Multiplayer messaging now starts only after the game has set up its network, instead of while mods are loading.

## v1.0.0

- First version: `/preorder` chat commands, +50% preorder fee, shopkeeper discounts, the confused shopkeeper, and the host-only / everyone setting.
